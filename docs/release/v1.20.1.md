# v1.20.1 - Evidence Planning Where Acceptance Needs It

Date: 2026-10-20

## What changed

- Astra's execution contract now connects primary inputs, material assumptions,
  methods and observable passing or disconfirming evidence when acceptance depends
  on an unverified assumption or an outcome beyond directly specified behavior.
  Examples include root-cause investigations, performance claims, research/data
  conclusions and semantic AI quality.
- Technical checks and demonstrated task outcomes remain separate. Model agreement
  alone cannot establish an outcome; unmeasured outcomes must be reported.
- Routine wording, layout and configuration edits, and known-cause repairs with
  clear expected results, keep their normal targeted checks unless they introduce
  such a claim. Selection depends on uncertainty and acceptance, not task size.
  No mandatory phase, document, reviewer, tool or approval gate was added.
- The test-group correction committed after v1.20.0 is included: `vibe-ps.mjs`
  belongs to the `provider-process` group, not also to `core`.

## Downstream impact

- Sync updates the harness-owned `.vibe/agent/astra-rules.md`. The existing
  model-registry eligibility and legacy workflow are unchanged.
- No runtime migration, schema, protocol or dependency change.
- Project-specific harness customizations still require conflict review during
  sync. Preserve local hybrid-file overrides, including hooks and document shards.
- The companion Codex global `limit-break` update is a personal installation
  change; it is not distributed by this repository or downstream sync.
